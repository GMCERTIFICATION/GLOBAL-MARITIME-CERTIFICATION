Client Document Upload & Data Security Notice

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This notice explains exactly how Global Maritime Certification Ltd ("GMC") receives, stores, and disposes of the personal documents you send us in connection with your maritime certification/endorsement request, and the security measures we apply.

1. Our website does not host any upload system

globalmaritimecertification.com is an informational site only — there is no document upload form, client portal, or login area on our website. You are never asked to upload sensitive documents directly to our site.

2. How your documents actually reach us

  1. First contact: you reach us via the WhatsApp button on our website, and we explain the service, requirements, and fees.
  2. Sending documents: you send your maritime documents to us by email, at info@globalmaritimecertification.com (hosted with IONOS).
  3. Secure storage: we transfer your documents into a private, dedicated Microsoft OneDrive folder for your case.
  4. Restricted access: we send you a unique link to that folder. The link and folder are accessible only to you and to GMC's two directors — no one else, and the folder is never made public or indexed.
  5. Payment: handled separately, by bank transfer, using coordinates we send you by email. No payment or card data is ever included in the document folder.
  6. Completion and deletion: once your documents are refrendated/endorsed by the relevant flag administration, we deliver the completed documents to you and permanently delete the case folder from OneDrive. This normally happens within 15 days, and no later than 30 days, after completion.

3. Security measures we apply

  • Documents are stored only in a private, access-restricted OneDrive folder created individually for each client — not in shared or public folders.
  • Access is limited to you and to GMC's two directors.
  • Folder links are unique per client and are not published, indexed, or reused.
  • Documents are deleted as soon as your case is complete, within the 15–30 day window described above.
  • Our business email runs on IONOS's professional email infrastructure.

4. What we ask of you

  • Send documents only through the email address confirmed by our team — info@globalmaritimecertification.com — and not through unofficial channels.
  • Keep your personal OneDrive link confidential; do not forward or share it with third parties.
  • Send us only the documents actually required for your certification/endorsement request.
  • Let us know promptly if you believe your documents or link may have been accessed without authorisation.

5. Retention and deletion

We do not keep your original documents any longer than necessary to complete your certification/endorsement. As described above, documents are deleted from OneDrive normally within 15 days of completion, and always within a maximum of 30 days. We retain only minimal case records (such as your name, contact details, and the service provided) for a reasonable period afterwards, for accounting and legal purposes.

6. Related policies

This notice should be read together with our Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about how your documents are handled, or wish to request deletion earlier than the standard timeline, contact us at info@globalmaritimecertification.com.
