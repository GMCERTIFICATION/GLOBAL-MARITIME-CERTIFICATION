Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Global Maritime Certification Ltd ("GMC", "we") uses cookies and similar technologies on globalmaritimecertification.com.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website, generally used to make websites work, remember your preferences, or gather usage statistics.

2. Our use of cookies

globalmaritimecertification.com is a purely informational website presenting our maritime documentation and certification services. It does not include contact forms, client logins, shopping carts, or payment processing, and it does not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

The WhatsApp button on our site simply opens a WhatsApp conversation with our team (via a standard wa.me link) — it does not itself set cookies on our site.

3. Essential/technical cookies

Our website is hosted as a static site (via GitHub Pages with a custom domain). The hosting provider may set a small number of strictly necessary technical cookies required for the site to load and function correctly and for basic security. These cannot be switched off, as they do not involve tracking and no consent banner is required for them under applicable law.

4. If this changes

Should we add tools such as website analytics (e.g. Google Analytics), a booking widget, or advertising pixels in the future, we will update this Cookie Policy accordingly and, where required by law, display a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies before they are set.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Even though our site currently sets no non-essential cookies, you can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Please note that blocking essential technical cookies may affect the way our website functions.

6. Contact

Questions about this Cookie Policy can be sent to info@globalmaritimecertification.com.
