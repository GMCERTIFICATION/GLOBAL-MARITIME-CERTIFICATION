Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Global Maritime Certification Ltd ("GMC", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of seafarers and other clients who contact us for maritime documentation and certification services. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

We serve clients internationally, including seafarers based in the European Union and the United Kingdom, and we apply the standards of the UK GDPR and, where our clients are located in the EU, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What personal data we collect

We only collect the data you choose to give us when you contact us. We do not use contact forms, cookies, or automated tracking to collect personal data through our website — the website is an informational brochure site only.

  • Identification & contact data: name, WhatsApp number, email address.
  • Professional/maritime documents: seafarer identity documents, certificates of competency, certificates of endorsement, and any other maritime documentation you send us for review, refrendation, or certification.
  • Communications: the content of your WhatsApp and email exchanges with us regarding your request.
  • Payment-related information: we do not collect card details. Payment is arranged by bank transfer, and we only send you our bank coordinates by email; we do not store your bank details.

3. How we collect it

You first contact us via the WhatsApp button on our website. We reply with information about our services, the documents required, and the applicable fee. You then send us your documents and any further correspondence by email to info@globalmaritimecertification.com.

PurposeLegal basis

5. How and where your documents are stored

Documents you send us by email are transferred to a private Microsoft OneDrive folder created specifically for your case. That folder is accessible only via a unique link shared exclusively with you and with GMC's two directors — no one else has access, and the folder is not publicly indexed or searchable.

Once your documents have been refrendated/endorsed by the relevant flag and the service is complete, we delete your documents from OneDrive and from our email records. This normally happens within 15 days of completion, and no later than 30 days.

6. Who we share your data with

  • The relevant maritime/flag administration, to the extent necessary to complete the certification or endorsement you requested.
  • Our service providers strictly for hosting purposes: Microsoft (OneDrive, for temporary secure document storage) and IONOS (our email provider).

We do not sell, rent, or use your personal data for marketing purposes, and we do not share it with any other third party.

7. International transfers

Microsoft OneDrive and IONOS may process data on servers located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or under equivalent data-protection safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as offered by those providers.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Documents you send us: deleted within 15 days (max. 30 days) of completion of your case, as described in Section 5.
  • Email correspondence and basic case records (name, contact details, dates, service provided): kept for a reasonable period after your case closes, for accounting and legal compliance purposes only.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure; restrict or object to processing; and request portability of your data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@globalmaritimecertification.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) or, if you are based in the EU, your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Our services are intended for professional seafarers and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
