What we do

Comprehensive maritime
compliance services

From individual seafarers to full fleet management — we handle every document, certification and registration with precision and speed.

📋
Maritime Documentation & Crew Endorsements
End-to-end support for seafarers' documentation and flag state requirements in full compliance with international standards.
  • Seaman's Books & Flag State Endorsements
  • Certificates of Competency (CoC) Recognition
  • GMDSS, Tanker & Specialized Endorsements
  • STCW Certification Support
🇵🇦
SEGUMAR Specialist — Panama
Comprehensive technical and regulatory support for Panama-flagged vessels — reliable interface with the Panama Maritime Authority.
  • Flag state regulatory & compliance support
  • Crew certification & endorsement coordination
  • Support for PSC inspections & deficiencies
  • Exemptions, dispensations & special authorizations
🚢
Ship Registration & Flagging
Specialized services for vessel registration under the Panama Flag — smooth and compliant throughout the entire flagging process.
  • Newbuild & initial vessel registration
  • Flag transfer & re-flagging operations
  • De-registration from previous registries
  • Coordination with classification societies
🌐
Certified Translation Services
Professional certified translations for all maritime and technical documentation — accuracy and compliance across jurisdictions.
  • STCW certificates, licenses & medical documents
  • Crew employment & contractual documentation
  • Technical & administrative maritime records
✅
Compliance Verification & Training Support
Structured verification and advisory services to support crew readiness and compliance with international maritime standards.
  • Verification of sea service & certification validity
  • Medical certificate validation
  • Guidance on mandatory STCW training
  • Support for advanced & safety-related courses
🤖
AI-Powered Assistant
Our intelligent assistant guides you through every procedure 24/7 in 6 languages. Instant answers, document checklists and automatic expiry reminders.
  • Available 24/7 in EN, ES, IT, DE, FR, 中文
  • Document checklists by flag & certificate type
  • Deadline guidance & process support
